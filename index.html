Events & Competitions

Choose from a wide range of technical and non-technical events to showcase your skills

Technical

CODE CLASH

A Two-Round Competitive Coding Challenge designed to test participants logical thinking, problem-solving ability, and programming skills through analytical and real-time coding tasks.

  • Duration: 1 Hours
  • Team Size: 1-2 Members
  • Prize: certificate
Technical

pixel to page

A Two-Round Digital Creativity Challenge focused on prompt design, visual imagination, and basic web development skills.

  • Duration: 1 Hour
  • Team Size: 2-4 Members
  • Prize: Certificate
Technical

Capture The Flag

A cybersecurity competition where participants solve security-related challenges to find hidden flags. Test your hacking skills in a controlled environment.

  • Duration: 4 Hours
  • Team Size: 1-3 Members
  • Prize: Certificate
Non-Technical

Upside-Down

Upside-Down is a fun two-member team event that tests presence of mind, communication, memory, and quick thinking through unique challenges

  • Duration: 1 Hours
  • Team Size: 2 Members
  • Prize: Certificate
Non-Technical

ADZAP

A fun two-round team guessing challenge where one player gives limited clues or acts out a picture while the partner guesses within one minute

  • Duration: 1 Hour
  • Individual Event
  • Prize: Certificate
Non-Technical

Design Hackathon

A creative design challenge where participants create UI/UX designs, posters, or digital art based on given themes and constraints.

  • Duration: 3 Hours
  • Team Size: 1-2 Members
  • Prize: Certificate
